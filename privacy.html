Privacy Policy

Forgotten Folklore Daily · last updated November 4, 2026

What this app is

"Forgotten Folklore Daily" is a private publishing tool run by the channel's owner. It only publishes the channel's own videos to the owner's own Facebook Page, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we collect

None from visitors or viewers. The tool stores only the access tokens of the owner's own accounts, on the owner's own computer, to publish videos. It does not read, store or share personal data of any other person, and it does not use cookies or analytics.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Videos are sent only to the platforms where they are published.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in Facebook Settings → Business integrations. Removing the app deletes every token it used. Questions: contact the Facebook Page "Forgotten Folklore Daily".
